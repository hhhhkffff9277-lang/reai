# Atomic Knowledge

## Definition

Atomic knowledge means small, reusable rules that survive across articles.

## What To Preserve

- Title patterns.
- Opening patterns.
- Share sentence structures.
- Image-use rules.
- Red-team lessons.
- Topic-selection lessons.

## Current Durable Rules

- A title should contain a concrete object plus entry aggression plus a judgment.
- The first paragraph should give a judgment.
- The first body sentence must not repeat the title.
- A share sentence should stand alone outside the article.
- A share sentence should help readers express judgment, foresight, distance, or institutional understanding.
- The default research lens is object, conflict, cost, mechanism, resource redistribution, and social implication.
- Prefer resource redistribution over pure technology-progress framing.
- Good covers communicate the article's main judgment by themselves.
- Do not promote, denigrate, or steer commercial behavior toward a specific company. Do not include trading-decision information.
- Do not add a new rule lightly. Improve an existing one first.

## Update Trigger

When the user says:

- "记住这个"
- "以后按这个来"
- "这个写法很好"

Suggest adding the smallest reusable version of that lesson to this reference, then edit only after the user confirms or clearly requests it.
